Privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Nest integration operated by the GitHub account merewords, configured as an OAuth application for Home Assistant.

Data access and use

With the account holder’s consent, the integration accesses supported Nest device identifiers, names, home or room information, status and events. Depending on granted permissions and device capabilities, this can include thermostat readings and controls, camera live streams and supported event images or clips. The purpose is household device monitoring, control and automation within a private Home Assistant installation.

Authentication and storage

Google handles sign-in. Home Assistant stores OAuth credentials and tokens needed to access authorised devices; the integration does not require the Google account password. Device history, events and media may be retained according to the operator’s recorder, media, automation and backup settings. Retention is controlled by those settings rather than a fixed period stated here.

Sharing

The integration is for personal household use. Google device data is not sold, used for advertising or published on this website. It is processed through Google services and the private Home Assistant installation. Authorised household users and services configured by the operator may access data to provide the requested functions.

The application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

The account holder can revoke access in Google Account connections and remove the Nest integration from Home Assistant. Revocation stops future authorised access, but does not automatically erase existing device history, media or backups. The administrator can delete those records separately.

Public website

These static pages have no forms, analytics scripts, advertising or embedded camera feeds. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for questions. Do not post credentials, tokens or private recordings in public GitHub issues. This policy should be updated if the integration’s data practices change.
